Before you turn the page.

Is everything really free?+

Yes. The app, backgrounds, and settings are free. There are no paid visual packs or subscriptions.

Which books can I use?+

BookTyper opens DRM-free EPUB files. It does not open encrypted Kindle or other protected ebooks, PDFs, or plain text files. A short original sample is included. Bring your own EPUBs or find books through Standard Ebooks.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. Once downloaded, the desktop app reads books and saves progress locally. You do not need to sign in or upload your books.

What about Mac, Linux, and mobile?+

The current download is for 64-bit Windows. Other platforms do not have a tested release yet. The small demo above runs in your browser, but it is not the full book reader.

Where is my progress saved?+

On Windows, progress and settings are stored in %APPDATA%\BookTyper. Keeping an EPUB at the same location lets BookTyper find its saved place.
